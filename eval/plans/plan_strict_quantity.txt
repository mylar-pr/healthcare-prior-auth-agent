Policy Number: SYN-EPO-7712 (fictional)

SECTION 1: INFLIXIMAB (REMICADE) COVERAGE

1.1 Infliximab is covered for moderate-to-severe Crohn's disease (ICD-10
K50.-) and ulcerative colitis (ICD-10 K51.-) confirmed by a
gastroenterologist.

1.2 Step therapy requirement: documented trial and inadequate response to at
least one conventional therapy (e.g. corticosteroids or an immunomodulator
such as azathioprine) for a minimum of 8 weeks, unless contraindicated.

1.3 Quantity limit: infliximab is limited to 2 vials (100mg each) per 8-week
infusion cycle after the induction series (weeks 0, 2, 6). Requests
exceeding this quantity require additional documentation of
dose-intensification medical necessity.

1.4 Prescriber requirement: must be prescribed by, or in consultation with,
a gastroenterologist.

SECTION 2: EXCLUSIONS

2.1 Self-administration is excluded; infliximab must be administered via
infusion in a licensed clinical setting.
