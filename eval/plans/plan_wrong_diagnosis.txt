## Coverage Criteria for Ustekinumab (Stelara)

Ustekinumab is covered exclusively for the following diagnoses, confirmed by ICD-10 code:
  - Plaque psoriasis (ICD-10 L40.0)
  - Psoriatic arthritis (ICD-10 L40.5-)

No other diagnoses are covered for this drug under this policy. Requests for
ustekinumab submitted with a diagnosis outside the above list will not be
approved under this coverage criterion, regardless of other clinical factors.

## Step Therapy

No step therapy is required for ustekinumab under this plan; it may be
prescribed as first-line biologic therapy for a covered diagnosis.

## Quantity Limits

Ustekinumab 90mg/1mL: limited to 1 syringe per 84-day (12-week) supply,
consistent with FDA-approved maintenance dosing.

## Prescriber Requirement

Must be prescribed by, or in consultation with, a dermatologist or
rheumatologist.
