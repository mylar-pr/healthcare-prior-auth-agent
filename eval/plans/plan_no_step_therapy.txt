CASCADE MUTUAL — RESPIRATORY BIOLOGIC BENEFIT POLICY
Plan: Cascade Mutual Advantage PPO (SYNTHETIC TEST DATA — not a real insurer)
Policy Number: SYN-PPO-6634 (fictional)

Section 1 — Dupilumab (Dupixent) Coverage

1.1 Dupilumab is covered for severe asthma with an eosinophilic phenotype or
oral corticosteroid-dependent asthma (ICD-10 J45.5-, J45.4-), and for
moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis (ICD-10 L20.-), when prescribed by or in
consultation with a pulmonologist, allergist/immunologist, or dermatologist.

1.2 No step therapy is required for dupilumab under this policy — it is
approved as an add-on biologic without a mandatory trial of another
controller therapy first, given its distinct mechanism of action.

1.3 Quantity limit: 2 pens (300mg each) per 28-day supply, matching the
FDA-approved every-other-week maintenance dosing after the initial loading
dose.

Section 2 — Exclusions

2.1 Use for mild, well-controlled asthma is excluded.
2.2 Use outside the FDA-approved age range for the specific indication is
excluded absent a documented medical exception.
