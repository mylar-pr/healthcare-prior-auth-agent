SYNTHETIC HEALTH PLAN — PHARMACY BENEFIT COVERAGE POLICY
Plan: Meridian Health Value PPO (TEST DATA — not a real insurer)
Policy Number: SYN-PPO-4471 (fictional)
Effective Date: 01/01/2026

SECTION 1: GENERAL PRIOR AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS

1.1 All specialty and biologic medications listed in Appendix A require prior
authorization before dispensing. Requests must include the prescribing
provider's clinical rationale, relevant diagnosis code(s), and documentation
of any required step therapy.

1.2 Prior authorization requests are reviewed within 5 business days for
standard requests and 24 hours for expedited/urgent requests.

SECTION 2: BIOLOGIC AGENTS FOR RHEUMATOLOGIC CONDITIONS

2.1 Adalimumab (Humira) and biosimilars are covered for the following
diagnoses when criteria in 2.2-2.4 are met:
  - Rheumatoid arthritis (ICD-10 M05.-, M06.-)
  - Psoriatic arthritis (ICD-10 L40.5-)
  - Ankylosing spondylitis (ICD-10 M45.-)
  - Crohn's disease (ICD-10 K50.-)
  - Ulcerative colitis (ICD-10 K51.-)
  - Plaque psoriasis (ICD-10 L40.0)

2.2 Step therapy requirement: Member must have documented trial and
inadequate response, intolerance, or contraindication to at least one
conventional disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (csDMARD), such as
methotrexate, for a minimum of 3 months, UNLESS a contraindication to all
csDMARDs is documented by the prescriber.

2.3 Quantity limit: Adalimumab is limited to 2 pens or syringes (40mg each)
per 28-day supply, consistent with FDA-approved dosing of one injection
every other week. Requests exceeding this quantity require additional
documentation of medical necessity (e.g. weekly dosing for Crohn's disease
induction).

2.4 Prescriber requirement: Initial requests must be submitted by, or in
consultation with, a rheumatologist, gastroenterologist, or dermatologist
as appropriate to the diagnosis.

SECTION 3: REAUTHORIZATION

3.1 Continuation of therapy requires documentation of clinical response
(e.g. improvement in tender/swollen joint count, reduction in CDAI/DAS28
score, or PASI improvement) at the first reauthorization, occurring no
later than 12 months after initial approval.

3.2 Lack of documented response at reauthorization may result in denial of
continued coverage pending step-down or alternative therapy discussion.

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS

4.1 Cosmetic use of any biologic agent is excluded from coverage under all
plan tiers.

4.2 Off-label use not supported by CMS-recognized compendia or peer-reviewed
literature is excluded absent a plan-approved medical exception.

SECTION 5: APPEALS

5.1 Members and prescribers may appeal an adverse determination within 180
days of notice. Expedited appeals are available when a delay would
jeopardize the member's health, as certified by the prescriber.
